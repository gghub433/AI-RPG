# Regions

Ten major regions, each with a visual theme, enemy roster, points of interest, and
story tie-in — matching the design brief's "fewer, detailed regions over empty
scale" rule.

1. **Hollow Reach Ruins** (Arc I, X, XII) — pre-Collapse/Varinthe ruin, fog +
   broken masonry theme. Enemies: Hollow Wisps, Grieving Wardens. Revisited three
   times across the game with new accessible depth each time.
2. **Veyth Hollow & the Dusk Fields** (Arc II) — rural border-town theme, warm
   lantern-lit interiors vs. amber dusk fields. Enemies: Hollow Rats, Dust Jackals.
3. **Aldenwatch Spire** (Arc III) — vertical trial-dungeon, Dominion military
   relic architecture reclaimed by Varinthe tech underneath. Enemies: Spire Wisps,
   Rustback Constructs; mini-boss Grieving Warden; boss Caelus.
4. **Caldrin Vane** (Arc IV, V, VII, IX) — the free city hub; Guild Quarter/Market
   Span/Lower Docks/Noble Terrace districts, each visually distinct (institutional
   stone, market canvas-and-timber, flooded brick docks, marble terrace respectively).
5. **Greywatch Pass** (Arc V) — mountain trade road, the Serah-betrayal confrontation
   setting; sparse, windswept, isolated — intentionally the loneliest-feeling region.
6. **Greyrot Expanse** (Arc VI) — Curse-touched wasteland, ash-grey palette with
   magenta Curse-mist accents. Enemies: Unburied archivist-constructs; boss Vharok.
   Contains the Varinthe Deep Archive sub-location.
7. **Harrow March** (Arc VII) — contested borderland, fortified pass battlefield
   aesthetic, banners of all three political factions visible simultaneously.
8. **The Ashgrave Remnant Camp** (Arc X) — hidden survivor settlement, deliberately
   small and intimate in contrast to Caldrin Vane's scale.
9. **The Rift Expanse** (Arc XI) — reality-torn region with two pocket-zone
   sub-areas (Mirror Reach, Still Hollow), visually defined by floating fractured
   geography and shifting skyboxes.
10. **The Source Chamber** (Arc XII) — final dungeon, beneath Hollow Reach;
    architecture that visually unifies every prior region's motifs (Varinthe glyphs,
    Dominion military relics, Curse-mist, rift-fracture) as a deliberate "everything
    led here" capstone design.

Each region additionally supports 2–4 side dungeons/secret areas for repeatable
endgame content (see `Docs/Design/04_LongTermProgression.md`), authored
incrementally rather than all at once per the "add quality content gradually" rule.
