# Extended NPC Roster (continued from 00_Companion_Roster.md)

Lighter-detail entries — still carrying every required field — for named NPCs who
anchor specific quests/regions but aren't primary companions.

### Isolde Rin
Age 33. Silver Moon Clan emissary. Appearance: silver ceremonial half-mask over one
eye (a Clan mourning tradition, not an injury). Voice: formal, precise, almost never
raises it. Personality: believes deeply in restraint as strength. History: lost an
older sibling to an uncontrolled anomaly surge a generation ago — the Clan's whole
caution-first doctrine traces back to that event. Motivation: seal the Varinthe
energy site, permanently. Personal quest: Arc VII political branch. Secret: the
sibling's death was partially the Clan's own doing (an early, botched anomaly
experiment), which she doesn't know yet. Relationship to Arden: respectful rival
negotiator; not romanceable. Consequence: if her branch is chosen in Arc VII, she
becomes a recurring strategic ally through Arc IX–X.

### Vash'tor Keel
Age 29. Demonic House envoy. Appearance: tailored, deliberately human-presenting
fashion (the Houses' younger generation reject old monstrous stereotypes on
principle). Voice: charming, transactional, genuinely means his offers even when
they sound too good. Personality: pragmatic broker, allergic to hoarding. History:
rose fast in House politics by brokering deals older Demonic nobles considered
beneath them. Motivation: keep House Keel relevant by staying indispensable to
everyone. Personal quest: Arc VII political branch. Secret: privately terrified the
Houses' human-presenting generation will be seen as weak by the old guard if a deal
goes bad. Relationship to Arden: transactional respect, no romance. Consequence: his
branch, if chosen, opens a unique recurring black-market vendor in Caldrin Vane.

### Noble-Marshal Edevane Thresh
Age 54. Dominion military-political figure. Appearance: parade-formal Dominion
uniform even in the field. Voice: measured, patriotic without irony. Personality:
genuinely believes centralized control prevents worse outcomes. History: career
Dominion officer, no personal anomaly history (deliberately — he's the "ordinary
institutional believer" among the three envoys). Motivation: Dominion control of the
Varinthe site as a stabilizing good. Personal quest: Arc VII political branch.
Secret: privately reports to Director Vorne, making the Dominion branch
retroactively more complicated if the player later opposes Vorne in Arc X–XII.
Relationship to Arden: cordial, institutional, not personal. Consequence: Dominion
branch support in Arc VII makes Arc X's Vorne confrontation harder (Thresh's
loyalty to Vorne complicates the Remnant Camp choice).

### Tobin Reyes
Age 23 (deceased, Arc I). Appearance: lanky, perpetually sunburned. Voice: nervous
jokes under pressure. Personality: good-hearted, not cut out for the Expedition's
real risks. History: Dray's least experienced scout, friends with Arden before Arc
I's events. Motivation (pre-death): wanted enough Expedition pay to send home to
Mira. Secret: none — his death is the one clean, blameless loss in the opening arc,
by design, to make Kade's and Reyes's own hesitant failure land harder than a
villain's clean murder would.

### Vharok, First Seal (NPC-as-boss, speaking antagonist)
Age: unknown, Varinthe containment-era construct. Voice: layered, multiple Varinthe
voices speaking slightly out of sync (a deliberate audio-design note for VO
direction). Personality: not malicious — executing a containment duty centuries
past its intended end. Motivation: contain the Curse-touched remnants it was built
to guard, including, eventually, the player. Consequence: defeating it (Arc VI) is
the first time the player kills something sympathetic rather than clearly hostile,
seeding the "not every enemy is evil" theme ahead of Arc VIII.

### Overseer-adjacent Guild Trainers (Caldrin Vane)
Eight interchangeable-but-individually-named Guild trainer NPCs — one per class —
offer the respec/trainer dialogue and class-specific flavor banter once a class is
chosen. Names and one-line personalities are catalogued in
`Docs/Design/01_Classes.md` under each class's "Guild Trainer" field rather than
duplicated here, since their only gameplay role is the skill-reset service.
