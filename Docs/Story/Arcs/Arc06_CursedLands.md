# Arc VI — The Cursed Lands

**Location:** The Greyrot Expanse (new region, 10-region count contributor). **Playtime target:** 4 hours.

## Narrative Goals
First major new-ability branch unlock (Curse-resistant skill tree nodes become
available Dominion-wide-rare), and the first hard confirmation that the Eclipse
System predates Arden by a long time — tying directly back to Arc I and Arc III's
pre-Collapse military lore.

## Key Scenes & Quests

- **Entry — "Past the Dead Line":** the Greyrot Expanse is a permanently
  curse-touched wasteland the Dominion quarantines; entry requires a
  companion-vouched Guild permit (ties back to Arc IV's guild rank system).
- **The Sunken Civilization ("Those Who Sealed Themselves"):** the region's core
  discovery — an underground civilization, the **Varinthe**, who deliberately walled
  themselves off centuries ago rather than let their own anomaly-research reach the
  surface. Their archive is the player's first real lore-dump location, delivered
  through readable logs and a non-combat NPC-spirit guide, **Ashlin Rell**, bound to
  the archive and unable to leave it.
- **New ability branch — "Curseweave":** Ashlin Rell teaches the player a
  Curse-resistance skill branch usable by any class, gated behind a trust-building
  mini-quest chain (3 short fetch/puzzle tasks proving the player "listens before
  taking"). This is the arc's version of a new mechanical system unlock.
- **Mini-boss — "The Unburied":** a corrupted Varinthe archivist-construct,
  Curse-type, whose gimmick is draining the player's mana directly rather than
  dealing health damage — teaches mana management under pressure.
- **Boss — "Vharok, First Seal":** the Varinthe's strongest containment guardian.
  Three-phase: Phase 1 standard; Phase 2 the arena floods with a damaging Curse mist
  that forces constant repositioning; Phase 3 Vharok fuses with the seal mechanism
  and must be fought while simultaneously avoiding being fully re-sealed with it
  (a scripted timer-pressure phase, generous enough not to feel like a fail-state
  trap). Defeating Vharok and choosing to absorb its core unlocks
  `fragment_varinthe_origin` — the first direct evidence that the Eclipse System is
  a *Varinthe* creation, not something native to Arden's new world either.

## Flags Set at Arc End
- `arc6_varinthe_discovered` (true)
- `arc6_curseweave_unlocked` (true)
- `arc6_fragment_varinthe_origin` (true if Vharok absorbed)

## Rewards
~1800 XP, Curseweave skill branch, Legendary-tier Curse-resistant armor piece from
Vharok, new region fully unlocked with 4 side dungeons for future repeat visits.
