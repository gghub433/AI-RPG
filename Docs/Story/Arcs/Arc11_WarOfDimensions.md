# Arc XI — War of Dimensions

**Location:** The Rift Expanse (new, reality-breached region) + traversal into two short "other-world" pocket zones. **Playtime target:** 5–6 hours.

## Narrative Goals
Escalate stakes to a multiversal scale, explain the Cult of the Hollow Depths'
actual goal, and introduce the game's hardest standard enemies ahead of the finale.

## Key Scenes & Quests

- **The Rift Opens:** Director Vorne's Directorate (Arc X), attempting to weaponize
  the Varinthe energy site from Arc VII against the Cult, instead tears open unstable
  rifts to adjacent realities. This is explicitly framed as Vorne's hubris causing a
  problem bigger than the one he was trying to contain — not a random cosmic event —
  keeping the plot's causality honest.
- **The Cult's actual goal, revealed:** the Cult of the Hollow Depths has been trying
  to *re-open* a Varinthe transfer gate on purpose, believing (correctly, per Arc VIII)
  that a "true anchor soul" the original System was calibrated for is still out there
  in another reality, and that retrieving it will let them control the System
  directly. This directly threatens Arden: if the Cult succeeds, the System may
  reassign primary compatibility away from him mid-game, which is dramatized as a
  real, visible Sync Rate volatility mechanic during this arc (Sync % visibly
  fluctuates during Cult ritual sequences).
- **Pocket zone 1 — "The Mirror Reach":** a short (45–60 min) alternate-reality zone
  where the Dominion won the war the Hollow Reach was named for, centuries ago, and
  Varinthe technology is openly, catastrophically militarized. Enemy roster: upgraded
  variants of Arc I/III enemies, now Curse-saturated. Delivers unique loot exclusive
  to this zone.
- **Pocket zone 2 — "The Still Hollow":** an alternate reality where the Hollow Reach
  anomaly was *never found* — Arden encounters a version of Thessaly Kade who never
  betrayed anyone, living a normal life, utterly unaware of everything the player has
  been through. This is the arc's emotional gut-punch scene and is entirely
  optional-but-strongly-signposted, rewarding exploration with the single most
  quietly devastating scene in the game rather than a combat reward.
- **Boss — "Vorne's Mistake" (The Rift Warden):** a non-malicious but lethally
  unstable construct born from the rift tear itself — fought as a race against the
  rift's own instability (environmental damage ramps over the fight's duration,
  pressuring the player toward decisive play rather than turtling).
- **Companion losses are possible here:** if any companion's loyalty/trust flags are
  low going into this arc's final rift-crossing sequence (tracked via each
  companion's own affinity threshold), they can be lost (written out, not killed
  outright except in one specific case — Kael Thorne, whose Silver Moon debt plot
  thread, if left completely unresolved since Arc VII, can cost him his life here).
  This is the direct payoff of the "consequences carry forward" story rule.

## Flags Set at Arc End
- `arc11_cult_goal_revealed` (true)
- `arc11_companion_losses` (list)
- `arc11_still_hollow_witnessed` (bool, optional)

## Rewards
~4000 XP, Mythical-tier Rift-exclusive gear, Secret Class unlock conditions become
checkable for the first time (see `Docs/Design/01_Classes.md`), full access to the
Rift Expanse as a late-game region with repeatable high-tier content.
